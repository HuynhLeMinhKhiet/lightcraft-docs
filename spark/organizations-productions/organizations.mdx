## Organizations and roles

Each member has an access level. It decides what they can open and change. A title, such as Director or Editor, only names their job.

| Access | What they can do |
|--------|------------------|
| **Owner** | Everything an Admin can, plus invite, change and remove members, manage billing and delete the organization. Each organization has one Owner. |
| **Admin** | Open every production, create productions and open **Settings**. |
| **User** | Open the productions shared with the whole organization, plus any they're given access to. |

## Your Organizations page

The Home page lists your recent scripts, then every organization you belong to under **Your Organizations**. Each card shows the Owner and how many members, productions, scripts and scenes it holds.

## Switch organizations

In the breadcrumb at the top, click the arrow beside the organization's name. The list shows each organization with your access and its member count. You can also open one from **Your Organizations** on the Home page.

## Organization Settings

Click **⋯** on the organization's card, then **Settings**. Admins and the Owner can open it.

<img
  src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/organization-settings.png"
  alt="Organization Settings. Box 1 is on Organization Name, 2 on Invite, 3 on the seat line, 4 on the Owner row and 5 on Billing."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

<div className="key">

1. **Organization Name**
2. **Invite**: Owner only
3. Seats in use
4. Members, with title and access
5. **Billing**: trial, seats, storage

</div>

Below Billing, **Delete Organization** removes the organization with all its productions, files and member access. Only the Owner can click **Delete permanently**.

<Warning>
  Deleting cancels a paid subscription at once, with no refund for the current period.
</Warning>

For members and seats, see [Members and seats](/spark/organizations-productions/members-and-seats).

## Leave an organization

Click **⋯** on the organization's card, then **Leave Organization**. Click **Leave** to confirm. The Owner can't leave their own organization, so the item is grayed out for them.
