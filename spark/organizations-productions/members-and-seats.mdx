Only the organization's Owner can invite, change and remove members. Everything here happens in **Organization Settings**, under **Members**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/invite-window.png"
  alt="The Invite member window. Marker 1 is on the free seats count, 2 on Add more user, 3 on Title, 4 on the access level, 5 on Production access and 6 on Invite."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '560px' }}
/>

1. Free seats left in the organization.
2. **Add more user** adds a row, to invite several people at once.
3. **Title**, such as Director. It only names the job.
4. **Admin** or **User**. This decides what they can open.
5. **Production access**, for people invited as a User.
6. **Invite** sends the invitations.

## Seats and billing

Each member uses one seat, and so does each invitation that hasn't been accepted yet. The seat line under **Members** shows the count, such as **2 of 3 seats used · 1 pending**.

When every seat is taken, the line turns red and asks you to add a paid seat from **Billing**. **Invite** is greyed out until then.

<img
  src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/seat-line-full.png"
  alt="The Members section of a full organization. Marker 1 is on the red seat line, 3 of 3 seats used, 2 pending. Marker 2 is on the greyed Invite button with the tooltip Seat capacity reached."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. The seat line of a full organization.
2. **Invite** stays greyed out until a seat is free.

**Billing** lists paid seats at $30 a month each, plus any free bonus seats. A new organization starts on a free trial, and nothing is charged until it ends.

<Warning>
  Files need an active subscription. If it ends, they lock at once and are deleted 30 days later. Subscribe again within those 30 days to get them back.
</Warning>

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite window">
    In **Organization Settings**, click **Invite** under **Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add each person">
    Type their email, pick a **Title**, and choose **Admin** or **User**. Click **Add more user** for another row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their productions">
    For a User, tick the productions they can open. Productions marked **Everyone** are open to every member already.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Click **Invite**. The invitation appears under **Members** as **Pending**, and holds a seat for 7 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Spark checks each address when you leave the box.

| Message | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Invalid email format** | The text isn't an email address. |
| **Already a member** | That person is in the organization. |
| **Invitation already sent** | That address has a pending invitation. |
| **Invitation expired**, with **Resend it** | That address's invitation ran out. Click **Resend it** to send it again. |

On a pending row, the envelope sends the invitation again. The **X** asks **Cancel this invitation?**; click **Cancel invitation** to free its seat.

An invitation that runs out moves under **Expired invitations**, and no longer holds a seat.

<img
  src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/expired-invitations.png"
  alt="The Members list. Marker 1 is on the seat line, 2 of 3 seats used, 1 pending. Marker 2 is on the Expired invitations group, and 3 on the Invite button."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. The seat line doesn't count the expired invitation.
2. The expired invitation, with its resend icon.
3. **Invite** works again.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="They accept while the organization is full">
    They aren't added. Spark shows that the organization is out of seats, and that their invitation stays valid until its last day. Once a seat is free, **Try again** lets them in.

</AccordionGroup>

## Change a member's access

Click the arrow at the end of a member's row, or of a pending invitation. **Production access** lists the productions they can be given.

<img
  src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/member-production-access.png"
  alt="The Production access window for one member. Marker 1 is on the list of productions with checkboxes, and 2 on Save Changes."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '520px' }}
/>

1. Ticked productions are the ones they can open.
2. **Save Changes** applies the new set.

Admins open every production, so their window has nothing to choose. The Owner can also change a member's access level and title on their row.

## Remove a member

On the member's row, click the remove icon (**Remove member**), then **Remove** to confirm. They lose access to the organization, and their seat is free again.
