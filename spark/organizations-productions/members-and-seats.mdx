Only the organization's Owner can invite, change and remove members. Everything here happens in **Organization Settings**, under **Members**.

**Add more user** adds a row, so you can invite several people at once. **Title**, such as Director, only names the job. **Admin** or **User** decides what they can open. **Production access** applies to people invited as a User.

## Seats and billing

Each member uses one seat, and so does each invitation that hasn't been accepted yet. The seat line under **Members** shows the count, such as **2 of 3 seats used · 1 pending**.

When every seat is taken, the line turns red and asks you to add a paid seat from **Billing**. **Invite** is grayed out until then.

<img
  src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/seat-line-full.png"

**Billing** lists paid seats at $30 a month each, plus any free bonus seats. A new organization starts on a free trial, and nothing is charged until it ends.

<Warning>
  Files need an active subscription. If it ends, they lock at once and are deleted 30 days later. Subscribe again within those 30 days to get them back.
</Warning>

## Invite a member

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Open the invite window">
    In **Organization Settings**, click **Invite** under **Members**.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/invite-button.png"
      alt="The Members section of Organization Settings, with a box on Invite."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add each person">
    Enter their email, pick a **Title**, and choose **Admin** or **User**. Click **Add more user** for another row.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/invite-add-person.png"
      alt="The Invite by email row. Box 1 is on the email field, 2 on Title, 3 on User and 4 on Add more user."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '600px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2 left">

    1. Email
    2. **Title**
    3. **Admin** or **User**
    4. **Add more user**

    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their productions">
    For a User, tick the productions they can open. Productions marked **Everyone** are open to every member already.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/invite-productions.png"
      alt="Production access in the invite window, with a box on the list of productions, both marked Everyone."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '600px' }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Click **Invite**. The invitation appears under **Members** as **Pending**, and holds a seat for 7 days.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/invite-send.png"
      alt="The bottom of the invite window, with a box on Invite."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '600px' }}
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>

Spark checks each address when you leave the field.

| Message | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Invalid email format** | The text isn't an email address. |
| **Already a member** | That person is in the organization. |
| **Invitation already sent** | That address has a pending invitation. |
| **Invitation expired**, with **Resend it** | That address's invitation ran out. Click **Resend it** to send it again. |

On a pending row, click the envelope to send the invitation again. To free its seat, click the **X**, then **Cancel invitation**.

An invitation that runs out moves under **Expired invitations**, and no longer holds a seat.

<img
  src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/expired-invitations.png"
  alt="The Members list. Box 1 is on the seat line, 2 of 3 seats used, 1 pending. Box 2 is on the Expired invitations group, and 3 on the Invite button."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-3">

1. Seat line
2. Expired invitation
3. **Invite**

</div>

The seat line doesn't count the expired invitation, so **Invite** works again. The expired invitation has a resend icon.

## Change a member's access

Click the arrow at the end of a member's row, or of a pending invitation. **Production access** lists the productions they can be given.

<img
  src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/member-production-access.png"
  alt="The Production access window for one member. Box 1 is on the list of productions with checkboxes, and 2 on Save Changes."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '520px' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-2 left">

1. Ticked productions
2. **Save Changes**

</div>

Tick the productions they can open, then click **Save Changes**.

Admins open every production, so their window has nothing to choose. The Owner can also change a member's access level and title on their row.

## Remove a member

On the member's row, click the remove icon (**Remove member**), then **Remove** to confirm. They lose access to the organization, and their seat is free again.
