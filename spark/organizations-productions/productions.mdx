A production holds one film, episode or commercial: its scripts, assets, takes and edits. Admins create productions, and choose who else can open each one.

<img
  src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/productions-overview.png"
  alt="An organization page. Marker 1 is on Search a production, 2 on New Production, 3 on a production card, 4 on its Access avatars and 5 on its more menu."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. **Search a production** filters the cards by name.
2. **New Production** creates one. Only Admins see it.
3. A production. Click it to open it.
4. **Access** shows who can open it.
5. **⋯** holds **Settings** and **Delete**.

## Productions and access

Each production is open to **Everyone in the organization**, or to **Only selected people**. Admins can open every production either way. A User opens a selected production only once someone gives them access.

## Create a production

On the organization page, click **New Production**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/create-production.png"
  alt="The Create New Production window. Marker 1 is on Production Name, 2 on Production Code, 3 on Camera Preset, 4 on Frame Rate and 5 on Access."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '520px' }}
/>

1. **Production Name**, required.
2. **Production Code**, a short code Spark fills in from the name's initials. You can change it.
3. **Camera Preset**, the camera body and sensor. Pick landscape or portrait beside it.
4. **Frame Rate**, 24fps or 25fps. It sets the timeline's frame grid and the frame rate on USD export.
5. **Access**, who can open the production.

You can also add a square **Production Image** of up to 10 MB. Click **Create Production** to finish.

## Set who can open a production

Click **⋯** on the production's card, then **Settings**. Under **Access**, choose **Everyone in the organization** or **Only selected people**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/organizations-productions/production-access.png"
  alt="The Access section of Production Settings. Marker 1 is on Only selected people, 2 on a ticked member and 3 on an Admin row without a checkbox."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '520px' }}
/>

1. **Only selected people** shows the member list.
2. A ticked member can open the production.
3. An Admin has no checkbox, because Admins open every production.

Click **Save Changes**. To set access for one person across productions, see [Change a member's access](/spark/organizations-productions/members-and-seats#change-a-members-access).

## Rename or delete a production

Click **⋯** on the production's card.

- To rename it, click **Settings**, change **Production Name**, then click **Save Changes**. The same window changes the camera preset and the frame rate.
- To delete it, click **Delete**, then **Delete** again to confirm.

<Warning>
  Deleting a production removes all its files and data for good. It can't be undone.
</Warning>
