## Get help in Spark

Click **Help** at the bottom left of any page in Spark. To ask about access, write to [support@lightcraft.pro](mailto:support@lightcraft.pro).

## Sign-in and access

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Spark says Email Not Whitelisted">
    Spark lets in approved email addresses only, and an invitation doesn't add yours. Write to [support@lightcraft.pro](mailto:support@lightcraft.pro), then sign in again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Spark Desktop says port 53682 is in use">
    Windows has reserved the port Spark Desktop signs in on. Open Command Prompt as Administrator and run `net stop winnat`. Sign in, then run `net start winnat`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't see New Production">
    Only Admins create productions. Ask your organization's Owner to change your access. See [Members and seats](/spark/organizations-productions/members-and-seats).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Invite is grayed out">
    Your seats are full. Add a seat in **Billing**, or cancel a pending invitation. See [Members and seats](/spark/organizations-productions/members-and-seats).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Scripts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A teammate sees an older script">
    Your edits are still a draft that only you see. Click **Publish changes** in the **Versioning** column. See [Drafts and versions](/spark/scripts/versioning).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The PDF preview stays empty and Upload is grayed out">
    Spark can't read a scan, or a PDF not laid out as a screenplay. Export the script from your screenwriting app as `.fountain` or `.fdx`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Locations and objects

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A location or object loads upside down or on its side">
    The file uses a different up axis. In the upload window, tick **Invert Up (Flip 180°)** or **Z-Up (Blender/3ds Max)**. See [Locations](/spark/scene-editor/locations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A performer looks giant or doll-sized in the location">
    The location's scale is wrong. Add the human figure to check it, then change **Scale** in **Location Properties**. See [Fix orientation or scale](/spark/scene-editor/locations#fix-orientation-or-scale).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An object seems to be missing">
    It may be very small or very large. Select it in the Outliner, then check **Scale** in **Object Properties**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Cameras and animation

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A camera or object doesn't keep its move">
    **Auto-keyframe** is off by default, so the move added no keyframe. Select the item, turn on **Auto-keyframe**, then move it again. See [Timeline and animation](/spark/setups-cameras/timeline-and-animation#animate-with-keyframes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A parented camera doesn't hold its position on the phone">
    This is a known issue that comes and goes, most often in a new 3D scene. Reload the scene, or switch to another camera and back. Also check that no two objects share a name.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## iPhone and iPad

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A 3D scene loads on the web but not on the phone">
    The location has more than about 5 million splats. Keep a smaller version for the app. See [Specs and limits](/spark/reference/specs-and-limits#size-limits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Web Follow doesn't engage">
    The link between the app and the browser sometimes needs a second try. Tap the lightning button again. See [Live camera sharing](/spark/spark-ios/live-camera-sharing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Driving across a large 3D scene is slow">
    Add a camera at each place the action happens, then jump between them. See [Camera setups on iPhone](/spark/spark-ios/setups-and-cameras-on-iphone).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The chroma key looks weak">
    The cause is usually the lighting or the picked color. Use **Pick color** on a cleaner patch of the screen, adjust **Clip black** and **Clip white** in **Tools**, or switch to **Person**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The phone gets hot">
    Frame rate drops as the device heats up. Turn on **Fast splat preview** and **Idle render skip**, render takes after the shoot, or use a phone cooler.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An older device keeps crashing">
    Older iPads have crashed repeatedly in the field. Use the newest iPhone or iPad you can for long shoots.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Take upload and sync

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A take doesn't appear in the Gallery">
    Takes stay on the device until you upload them. Upload from the app's Takes page, or use Spark Desktop. See [Upload takes from iPhone](/spark/gallery/spark-desktop).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Spark Desktop says Apple Mobile Device Service not found">
    Windows needs Apple Devices first. Install it from the Microsoft Store, open it once, then click **Pull takes** again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Review and edit

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Editor says Nothing playable to mark">
    The CG Take is a still, because its camera doesn't move. Insert it without marks, or add keyframes to the camera and refresh its preview.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
