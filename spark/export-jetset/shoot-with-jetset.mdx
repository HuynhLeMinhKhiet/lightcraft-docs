## Spark iOS app or Jetset

Use the Spark iOS app to film on an iPhone inside the 3D scene. See [Composite and record](/spark/spark-ios/composite-and-record). Use [Jetset](/tutorials/jetset/introduction-jetset) when a cinema camera is on set. Jetset tracks it, and Autoshot brings the tracking into Blender, Unreal, After Effects or Nuke.

## Export to Jetset

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Export the scene">
    In the scene editor, click **Export**, then **Export to Jetset**. Spark packs the scene and uploads it. P5 stairwell took about 15 seconds.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/export-jetset/exporting-scene.png"
      alt="The Exporting Scene window at 6%, reading Processing Motorcycle.usdz."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '420px' }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Scan the code with your device to open the scene in Jetset. The link expires in 5 minutes.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/export-jetset/jetset-qr.png"
      alt="The Scan to Open in Jetset window for P5 stairwell, with the QR code blurred. Below it: Scan this QR code with your device to open in Jetset. Link expires in 5 minutes."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '380px' }}
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>

To scan again later, click **Export**, then **Export History**. Each export lists its date and size. Click **QR Code** for a fresh code.

<img
  src="/images/spark/export-jetset/export-history.png"
  alt="The Export History window with one P5 stairwell export, Oct 29, 2026, 8:39 PM, 42.7 MB, and a box on its QR Code button."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '500px' }}
/>

## Download a 3D scene as USDZ

Click **Export**, then **Download USDZ**. Spark runs the same export and saves the scene as one file, such as `P5 stairwell.usdz`. P5 stairwell came to 44.7 MB.
