---
title: 'Live camera sharing'
description: 'Share your iPhone or iPad camera, and follow it from the browser.'
---

Live camera sharing lets people in the browser watch your framing as you move the phone. The browser calls it **Direct Drive**. Spark sends the camera's position, not video, so the browser draws the view itself.

## Broadcast your camera

1. Open the 3D scene on the phone and keep the app open.
2. Tap the lightning button in the bottom bar. It turns green and shows **Web Follow engaged!**

<img
  src="/images/spark/spark-ios/web-follow.png"
  alt="The Web Follow engaged message above the bottom bar, with a box on the green lightning button."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '400px' }}
/>

Several people can broadcast at once. Each broadcaster shows as a gray camera in the 3D scene.

## Follow a live camera

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Open the same 3D scene in the browser">
    Sign in to the web app and open the scene the phone is in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Direct Drive">
    On the camera preview, click the lightning button. Its tooltip counts who's available, such as **1 cinematographer available**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Follow">
    Click **Follow** beside a name. The camera preview now moves with that phone.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/spark-ios/direct-drive-list.png"
      alt="The Direct Drive panel listing one active cinematographer on an iPhone, with the surname blurred. Box 1 is on the lightning button and 2 on Follow."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '425px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2 left">

    1. Lightning button
    2. **Follow**

    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

To stop, click **Stop Following** in the same panel.

<img
  src="/images/spark/spark-ios/direct-drive-following.png"
  alt="The Direct Drive panel reading Following, with the surname blurred, and a box on Stop Following."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '425px' }}
/>

If the panel reads **No cinematographers broadcasting**, open the app on the phone, in this scene, and tap the lightning button again.
