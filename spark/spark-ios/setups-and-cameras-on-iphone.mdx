## Jump to a setup or camera

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Open Setups & Cameras">
    Tap the list button in the bottom bar, then **2 Setups**. The number is how many setups the 3D scene has.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/spark-ios/setups-panel.png"
      alt="Setups & Cameras. Box 1 is on Setup B, orange with a tick, 2 on the Camera 2 chip, 3 on 2 Setups and 4 on the list button."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '400px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2 left">

    1. Setup
    2. Camera
    3. **2 Setups**
    4. List button

    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a setup">
    The setup turns orange with a tick. You stand in its arrangement without jumping to a camera.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tap a camera">
    The view jumps to that camera and its chip turns blue. The picker beside **Record** shows the setup and camera, such as **B 2**. Slide the row sideways to see the rest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a stationary camera

Tap the blue **+** at the far right of the row. Spark adds a camera at your position, in the setup you're in, and selects it. Use one to mark a spot in a large scene and jump back later.

<img
  src="/images/spark/spark-ios/add-stationary.png"
  alt="The Setups & Cameras row slid to its end, with a box on the blue plus chip at the far right."

/>

## Ride a moving camera

Tap a camera with a path, then tap play. The view rides the path you set in the web app, and you keep handheld control of the framing.

## Camera motion types

Each camera's icon shows how it moves. You set paths and parents in the [web app](/spark/setups-cameras/cameras).

<img
  src="/images/spark/spark-ios/motion-types.png"
  alt="Two Camera 2 chips. Box 1 is on the one with speed lines on its icon, and 2 on the one with a plain icon."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '400px' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-2 left">

1. **Path**
2. **Stationary**

</div>

| Type | Camera |
|---|---|
| **Stationary** | Plain icon. Holds one position |
| **Path** | Speed lines on the icon. Follows keyframes |
| **Parented** | Icon tethered to an object. Rides an object or a part of one, or tracks a target |

## Hidden and moved parts

Parts you hide or move in the web app look the same in the app. Each setup shows its own part changes, and a change reaches an open scene within a few seconds. See [Parts of an object](/spark/scene-editor/parts-of-an-object).
