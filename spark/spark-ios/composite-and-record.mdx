## Turn on compositing and key a performer

Tap **Off** at the top. The **CAMERA/KEY** menu opens.

<img
  src="/images/spark/spark-ios/key-menu.png"

  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '340px' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-2 left">

1. Camera button
2. **CAMERA/KEY**

- **Person**: cuts the performer out of the room, with no green screen
- **Chroma**: keys out a green or blue screen, for a cleaner edge
- **Off**: records the 3D scene with no camera

With **Chroma**, tap **Pick color**, then tap the screen.

<img
  src="/images/spark/spark-ios/chroma-row.png"
  alt="Chroma on, with the message Tap the green or blue screen to set the key color. Box 1 is on Pick color, 2 on Match floor with a tick and 3 on Find Screen."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '400px' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-3 left">

1. **Pick color**
2. **Match floor**
3. **Find Screen**

</div>

- **Find Screen** finds the edges of a small screen, and the 3D scene fills everything outside it.
- **Clip black** and **Clip white** in **Tools** fine-tune the key.

Tap **Tools** to switch views and set what shows on screen.

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Comp** | The performer over the 3D scene |
| **Cam** | The raw camera |
| **Depth** | Depth for each frame |
| **Matte** | The matte used for the key |

| Switch | Does |
|---|---|
| **Show Virtual World** | Off shows the full camera view, and the floor grid stays |
| **Floor grid** | Shows the grid on screen. It's never recorded |
| **Match Floor uses splat** | Off assumes the scene's floor is at height 0 |

## Place a performer

Point the camera at the real floor and tap **Match floor**. It measures the distance and sets your height, then shows a tick. It can't run while you're locked to a camera.

Tap **Align** to seat the performer in the scene, such as behind a desk or in a car seat.

## Record a take

1. Pick the setup and camera. See [Camera setups on iPhone](/spark/spark-ios/setups-and-cameras-on-iphone).
2. Tap **Record**. The button shows the time and the take number.
3. Tap it again to stop.

<img
  src="/images/spark/spark-ios/record.jpg"
  alt="Recording in P5 stairwell, with a box on the red button reading 00:05, Take 1."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '360px' }}
/>

Takes number themselves in each setup. A take with the camera off reads **No camera**, and shows **BG ONLY** in the Gallery.

<Note>
Recording claims the camera, so screen sharing over Zoom or Meet stops until the take ends.
</Note>

## Record offline, sync later

A take recorded offline saves on the iPhone. It syncs to the web app when the iPhone reconnects.

## Take files on the device

Each take keeps its elements as separate files, sorted by production, then script scene.

| File prefix | Contents |
|---|---|
| `cam_*` | Raw camera footage |
| `comp_*` | The live composite |
| `depth_*` | Depth for each frame |
| `tracking_*.json` | 3D camera tracking |
| `meta_*.json` | Take metadata |

<Warning>
Takes stay on the device until they're uploaded. An app update is safe, but deleting the app may not be.
</Warning>
