The Spark iOS app turns an iPhone or iPad into a camera inside your 3D scenes. It opens the same scenes, setups and cameras as the web app. To install it, see [Install and sign in](/spark/get-started/install-and-sign-in#install-and-sign-in-on-iphone-or-ipad).

## What the app does

You film takes inside a 3D scene. The app can also share your camera with people in the browser. Tap the view to hide the controls and see the whole frame.

The bottom bar holds the rest:

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| Lightning | Shares your camera. See [Live camera sharing](/spark/spark-ios/live-camera-sharing) |
| Camera | Shows the lens, focus and T-stop controls |
| List | Shows **Setups & Cameras** and **Script** |
| Gear | Shows the view settings |
| ? | Links to the Discord |

## Open a 3D scene

1. Tap your organization, then a production, then a script.
2. Tap a scene heading. A blue cube shows how many 3D scenes it has.
3. Tap **View in 3D**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/spark-ios/open-script.png"
  alt="The Rooftop Test script in the app with INT. STAIRWELL - DAWN open, the author's surname blurred, and a box on View in 3D on the P5 stairwell card, which reads 2 Shots."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '360px' }}
/>

The app counts a 3D scene's cameras as **Shots**. Each scene loads with its locations, setups and cameras from the web app.

<Note>
The app takes about 5 million splats per scene. Above that, the scene won't load, so keep a smaller version for the app.
</Note>

## Browse a script

Tap the script button at the top left to read the script and open another 3D scene. Tap the clapperboard for Takes. Lists you make in the Gallery don't show in the app.

## Move through a scene

Use the two joysticks to move and turn, or drag on the view to look around. Tap a dial to open its scale:

- **Speed**: how fast the joysticks move you, 3.0 m/s to begin with
- **Height**: how far above the floor the camera sits, in meters

Tap **AR** to fly by moving the iPhone itself. Raise **Speed** to cover a large scene from your chair.

## Camera settings

Tap the camera in the bottom bar. Slide the ruler to set the selected control.

Tap the camera's name to open **Camera & Capture**. **Simulated camera** is the look you're matching. **Capture quality** is what the iPhone records. Picking a camera overrides the default on this device only.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="View settings">
    | Setting | Does |
    |---|---|
    | **Center mark** | Marks the exact center of the frame. It's never recorded |
    | **Fast splat preview** | Draws splats at lower quality for a faster, cooler preview. Recorded comps keep full quality |
    | **Idle render skip** | Reuses the last 3D render while the camera is still, which runs cooler on a tripod. Turn it off if you see compositing glitches |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
