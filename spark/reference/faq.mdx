## Get started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does Spark cost?">
    A new organization starts on a free trial. After that, paid seats cost $30 a month each. See [Members and seats](/spark/organizations-productions/members-and-seats).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do I need to run Spark?">
    Google Chrome for the web app, an iPhone or iPad to shoot, and Spark Desktop to move takes off the device. See [Install and sign in](/spark/get-started/install-and-sign-in).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I sign in?">
    Spark lets in approved email addresses only. See the fix in [Troubleshooting](/spark/troubleshooting/troubleshooting#sign-in-and-access).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can invite people?">
    Only the organization's Owner. See [Members and seats](/spark/organizations-productions/members-and-seats).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see New Production?">
    Only Admins create productions. See the fix in [Troubleshooting](/spark/troubleshooting/troubleshooting#sign-in-and-access).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Scripts and 3D scenes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why didn't my script create any 3D scenes?">
    Scene headings create script scenes. You create each 3D scene yourself. See [3D scenes](/spark/scene-editor/3d-scenes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't my team see my script edits?">
    Your edits stay a draft until you publish them. See [Drafts and versions](/spark/scripts/versioning).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my own 3D files?">
    Yes. Upload splats and USD files to the Asset Library. See [Specs and limits](/spark/reference/specs-and-limits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add my own lights?">
    No. Every 3D scene uses the same fixed lighting. See [Scene lighting](/spark/scene-editor/lights-and-environment).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Takes and edits

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a take and a CG Take?">
    A take is a recording from the Spark iOS app. Spark renders a CG Take from a camera's move in the browser. See [Review and share takes](/spark/gallery/gallery#takes-and-cg-takes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a green screen?">
    No. **Person** cuts the performer out of the room, and **Chroma** gives a cleaner edge with a green or blue screen. See [Composite and record](/spark/spark-ios/composite-and-record).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why isn't my take in the Gallery?">
    Takes stay on the device until you upload them. See the fix in [Troubleshooting](/spark/troubleshooting/troubleshooting#take-upload-and-sync).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I share takes with someone outside Spark?">
    Yes. Put them in a list and share its link, with a password if you like. See [Share a list with a link](/spark/gallery/gallery#share-a-list-with-a-link).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cut my takes in Spark?">
    Yes. The Editor cuts takes into an edit and exports an MP4. See [Editor](/spark/editing/editor).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I shoot with a cinema camera?">
    Export the 3D scene to Jetset, then scan its QR code. See [Shoot with Jetset](/spark/export-jetset/shoot-with-jetset).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
