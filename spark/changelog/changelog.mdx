<Update label="2026-10-20" tags={["Web"]}>

### Your storage limit now applies to every upload

Once your organization reaches its storage limit, new uploads are paused everywhere — files, shot previews, editor exports and takes — until you free up space or add storage. An upload you start while under the limit always finishes, even if it takes you over. Bulk upload warns you before a batch would cross the limit.

Various bug fixes and improvements (3 changes).

</Update>
