A 3D scene is a set you build for one script scene. A script scene can hold several 3D scenes, each with its own location, objects and cameras.

</div>

## Create a 3D scene

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Pick the script scene">
    On the script page, click the script scene in the left column. Its 3D scenes show on the right.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/scene-editor/create-pick-scene.png"
      alt="The Script scenes column, with a box on INT. PARK - DAY and its 3D scene count."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '560px' }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create a new 3D scene">
    The **Create 3D Scene** window opens. Spark fills in the name from the scene heading.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/scene-editor/create-new-button.png"
      alt="The right column of the script page, with a box on Create a new 3D scene."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '680px' }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the camera">
    Pick a **Camera Preset**, then landscape or portrait.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/scene-editor/create-3d-scene.png"
      alt="The Create 3D Scene window. Box 1 is on Camera Preset with Landscape and Portrait, and 2 on Create. The name reads INT. PARK - DAY."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '560px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2 left">

    1. Camera preset and orientation
    2. **Create**

    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create**. Spark shows **Scene created!** and the new card starts with 1 setup, 1 camera and 1 take.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/scene-editor/scene-created.png"
      alt="The script page after creating a 3D scene. Box 1 is on the Scene created! message and 2 on the new P5 park test card, with 1 setup, 1 camera and 1 take."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2">

    1. **Scene created!**
    2. New 3D scene card

    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open a 3D scene

Click its card. The scene editor opens on the **3D Scene** tab. The card shows when you last opened it, such as **Opened 3 minutes ago**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/open-3d-scene.png"

/>

## Scene navigation

**Scene navigation** (☰, top left) moves between 3D scenes without leaving the scene editor. It lists the script's scene headings and the 3D scenes of the selected heading.

Click a 3D scene to open it, or click **New 3D Scene** to add one to that heading.

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/scene-navigation.jpg"

## Rename, clone or delete a 3D scene

Hover the card, then click **⋯**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/card-menu.png"
  alt="A 3D scene card with its menu open and boxed: Settings, Clone and Delete."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '520px' }}
/>

| Item | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Settings** | Opens **3D Scene Settings**: the name, the camera preset, and landscape or portrait. Click **Save**. |
| **Clone** | Makes a copy named **Clone -** and the original name. Change the name, then confirm. |
| **Delete** | Deletes the 3D scene, with its cameras, keyframes, audio clips and other data. It can't be undone. |

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/scene-settings.png"
  alt="The 3D Scene Settings window. Box 1 is on Camera Preset with Landscape and Portrait, 2 on Name, 3 on the Danger Zone with Delete 3D Scene, and 4 on Save."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '540px' }}
/>

## Move a 3D scene to another heading

Drag the card by its handle (⠿, top left) onto another script scene.

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/move-3d-scene.jpg"
  alt="The script page. Box 1 is on the handle at the top left of a 3D scene card, and 2 on the list of script scenes on the left."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-2">

1. Card handle
2. Script scenes

</div>

When you publish script edits that rename a heading, Spark asks where the 3D scenes go. The **Before publishing: where do these 3D scenes go?** window lists each changed heading and pre-picks the new one.

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/publish-move-scenes.png"
  alt="The Before publishing window. Box 1 is on the changed heading, INT. STAIRWELL - NIGHT to INT. STAIRWELL - DAWN, 2 on Publish, decide later and 3 on Publish and move scenes."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-3">

1. Old and new heading
2. **Publish, decide later**
3. **Publish & move scenes**

</div>

- **Publish & move scenes** moves the 3D scenes to the headings shown.
- **Publish, decide later** publishes and leaves them without a heading. Drag them back later.
