---
title: 'Scene lighting'
description: 'Every 3D scene uses the same fixed studio lighting. There are no lights to add.'
---

Every 3D scene is lit by one fixed environment, Lightcraft's **Spark Studio** lighting. You can't add lights or pick another environment, so a scene looks the same in the Spark web app and the Spark iOS app.

## The fixed studio lighting

- The Outliner has no **Lights** row. You can't add a directional, spot or point light.
- There's no environment picker and no brightness control.
- The lighting falls on USD objects only. A Gaussian splat keeps the lighting it was scanned or rendered with.

Lightcraft is working on adjustable lighting. Relighting splats is a fast-moving field, and Spark will add it once an approach is fast and easy enough.

<Tip>
  To change the look, bake it into the asset. Light the splat before you scan or render it, or give a USD object its own material.
</Tip>
