The bottom of the Outliner holds three helpers: **Locators**, **Audio** and **Rulers**. Each row has a **+** that adds one.

1. **Locators**, marked points in the scene.

## Add audio

Sound goes in two stages: you add the file to the scene, then place it on a Timeline track.

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Add the sound to the scene">
    Click **+** on the **Audio** row. In the audio library, hover a sound and click **Add to Scene**. It shows under **Audio** with its length.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a track">
    On the Timeline, click **+** on the **Audio** row. Spark adds a track named **Audio 1**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the clip">
    Move the playhead to where the sound starts. On the sound's row in the Outliner, click **+** (**Add to timeline at playhead**).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/audio-track.png"
  alt="The Timeline with the Audio row. Marker 1 is on the Audio plus button, and 2 on the P5 tone clip on track Audio 1, with its waveform."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. **+** adds an audio track.
2. The clip on its track.

Click the clip to set its **Start Time**, **Trim Start**, **Trim End** and **Volume**. Click the track for **Track Name**, **Track Volume** and **Muted**.

To upload a sound, click **Add to Library** in the audio library. Spark reads its length, format and size. Audio files can be `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.ogg`, `.m4a`, `.aac` or `.flac`.

## Use locators

A locator marks a point in the scene, such as where an actor stands. Locators are saved in the USD file when you export the scene.

1. Click **+** on the **Locators** row.
2. Click the spot on the 3D canvas.

**Locator Properties** holds its **Name**, **Position**, and its rotation as **Yaw (Y)** and **Pitch (X)**. A locator without a name shows as **Untitled Locator**.

## Measure with rulers

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Pick the unit">
    Click the unit button on the **Rulers** row, then **Meters (m)** or **Feet (ft)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a ruler">
    Click **+** on the **Rulers** row, then click two points on the 3D canvas. The length shows on the canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust it">
    Drag either end with its gizmo, or type **Start** and **End** values in **Ruler Properties**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/ruler-properties.png"
  alt="Ruler Properties for Ruler 1. Marker 1 is on the ft unit button on the Rulers row, and 2 on Distance, 0.401 ft. Start and End positions sit below."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '290px' }}
/>

1. The unit button.
2. **Distance**, the ruler's length.

To delete a locator or a ruler, hover its row, click the trash, then confirm.
