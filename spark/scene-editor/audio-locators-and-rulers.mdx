## Add audio

Sound goes in two stages: you add the file to the scene, then place it on a Timeline track.

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Add the sound to the scene">
    Click **+** on the **Audio** row. In the audio library, hover a sound and click **Add to Scene**. It shows under **Audio** with its length.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/scene-editor/audio-add.jpg"
      alt="The scene editor. Box 1 is on the plus button of the Audio row in the Outliner, and 2 on the plus button of the Audio row on the Timeline."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2">

    1. Audio **+** in the Outliner
    2. Audio **+** on the Timeline

    </div>

    <img
      src="/images/spark/scene-editor/audio-library-card.png"
      alt="The audio library with the pointer on the P5 tone card, and a box on its Add to Scene button."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '640px' }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a track">
    On the Timeline, click **+** on the **Audio** row. Spark adds a track named **Audio 1**.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/scene-editor/audio-track.png"
      alt="The Timeline track list, with a box on the plus button of the Audio row."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '600px' }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the clip">
    Move the playhead to where the sound starts. On the sound's row in the Outliner, click **+** (**Add to timeline at playhead**).

    <img
      src="/images/spark/scene-editor/audio-clip.jpg"
      alt="The scene editor with the P5 tone clip selected. Box 1 is on the P5 tone row in the Outliner, 2 on the clip on the Timeline, 3 on the track Audio 1 and 4 on Audio Clip Properties."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2">

    1. Sound row
    2. Clip
    3. Track
    4. **Audio Clip Properties**

    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click the clip to set its **Start Time**, **Trim Start**, **Trim End** and **Volume**. Click the track for **Track Name**, **Track Volume** and **Muted**.

To upload a sound, click **Add to Library** in the audio library. Spark reads its length, format and size. Audio files can be `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.ogg`, `.m4a`, `.aac` or `.flac`.

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/audio-settings.jpg"
  alt="The Audio Settings window for p5-tone. Box 1 is on the Preview player, 2 on Audio name and Tags, 3 on Audio Info and 4 on Add to Library."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-2">

1. **Preview**
2. **Audio name** and **Tags**
3. **Audio Info**
4. **Add to Library**

</div>

## Use locators

A locator marks a point in the scene, such as where an actor stands. Locators are saved in the USD file when you export the scene.

1. Click **+** on the **Locators** row.
2. Click the spot on the 3D canvas.

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/locator.jpg"
  alt="The scene editor with a locator placed. Box 1 is on the plus button of the Locators row, 2 on the locator and its gizmo in the canvas, and 3 on Locator Properties with Name, Position, Yaw and Pitch."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-3">

1. Locators **+**
2. Locator
3. **Locator Properties**

</div>

**Locator Properties** holds its **Name**, **Position**, and its rotation as **Yaw (Y)** and **Pitch (X)**. A locator without a name shows as **Untitled Locator**.

## Measure with rulers

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Pick the unit">
    Click the unit button on the **Rulers** row, then **Meters (m)** or **Feet (ft)**.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/scene-editor/ruler.png"
      alt="The Rulers row and Ruler Properties for Ruler 1. Box 1 is on the ft unit button, 2 on the Rulers plus button, 3 on Distance, 0.401 ft, and 4 on the Start and End positions."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '290px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2 left">

    1. Unit button
    2. Add a ruler
    3. **Distance**
    4. **Start** and **End**

    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a ruler">
    Click **+** on the **Rulers** row, then click two points on the 3D canvas. The length shows on the canvas.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/scene-editor/ruler-placed.png"
      alt="A ruler on the 3D canvas. Box 1 is on its length label, 0.401 ft, and 2 on the ruler between its two ends."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '540px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2 left">

    1. Length
    2. Ruler

    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust it">
    Drag either end with its gizmo, or type **Start** and **End** values in **Ruler Properties**.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/scene-editor/ruler-adjust.jpg"
      alt="The scene editor with Ruler 1 selected. Box 1 is on the gizmo at one end of the ruler, and 2 on the Start and End values in Ruler Properties."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2">

    1. End gizmo
    2. **Start** and **End**

    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>
