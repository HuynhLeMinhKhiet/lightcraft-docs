The scene editor is the **3D Scene** tab, where you build a 3D scene. The 3D canvas fills the middle, the Outliner and Properties panel sit on the right, and the Timeline runs along the bottom.

## 3D canvas and toolbar

The 3D canvas is the view you fly through and click in. Its toolbar runs along the top left.

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/canvas-toolbar.png"
  alt="The canvas toolbar, with a numbered box on each of its seven buttons: a gear, a compass, a square, a bug, an arrow down, a house and a keyboard."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '620px' }}
/>

<div className="key left">

1. **Camera Controls**
2. **Show Gizmo**
3. **Hide Ground Plane**
4. **Show Debug Info**
5. **Top-Down View**
6. **Reset view**
7. **Keyboard shortcuts**

</div>

- **Camera Controls** sets your flying speed. See [Fly through a 3D scene](/spark/scene-editor/move-around-a-scene#fly-through-a-3d-scene).
- **Top-Down View** looks straight down.
- **Reset view** brings the view back to where it started.

When you select an item, the move, rotate and scale buttons show at the top right. See [Move, rotate or scale](/spark/scene-editor/objects#move-rotate-or-scale).

## Camera preview

The camera preview shows the view through the selected camera, with its lens below, such as **16:9, 20mm**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/camera-preview.jpg"
  alt="The 3D canvas with the camera preview at the bottom right. Box 1 is on the enlarge button in the preview's header, and 2 on the minus button beside it."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-2">

1. Enlarge (**Shift+F**)
2. **—** Fold away

</div>

- Press **Shift+F**, or click the enlarge button, to make it larger over the canvas. Press **Shift+F** again to shrink it.
- Click **—** to fold it into a **Camera 1** chip at the bottom right. Click the chip to bring it back.

## Outliner

The Outliner lists everything in the 3D scene, in rows you can fold.

| Row | Holds |
|-----|-------|
| **Locations** | The scene's one location. |
| **Base Setup** and other setups | The objects and cameras in each setup, grouped by kind, such as **VEHICLES**, **PROPS** and **CAMERAS**. |
| **Locators** | Marked points. See [Audio, locators and rulers](/spark/scene-editor/audio-locators-and-rulers). |
| **Audio** | Sound files added to the scene. |
| **Rulers** | Measurements, in meters or feet. |

Hover an item's row to show its buttons: an eye to hide it, **Replace file**, and a trash to delete it. A setup row has three buttons: **Add an object to this setup**, **Add a camera to this setup** and **Clone this setup**.

## Properties panel

The Properties panel shows the settings of the item you selected, such as **Object Properties** or **Location Properties**. With nothing selected, it reads **Select a node to edit its properties.**

## Script tab

The **Script** tab shows the text of the script scene this 3D scene belongs to. Click **Outliner** to go back.
