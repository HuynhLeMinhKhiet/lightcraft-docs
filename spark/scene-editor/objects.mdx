Objects are the USD models you place in a location: props, vehicles and characters. Each object belongs to a setup, and shows under that setup in the Outliner.

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/object-selected.jpg"
  alt="The scene editor with the P5 bike object selected in the hall. Marker 1 is on the move, rotate and scale buttons, 2 on the object's row in the Outliner, 3 on Remove from Scene, 4 on Name and 5 on the Replace and settings buttons."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. **Position**, **Rotation** and **Scale**, for the gizmo.
2. The object's row in the Outliner, under its setup and kind.
3. **Remove from Scene**.
4. **Name**.
5. **Replace with another file** (↻), and the file's settings (⚙).

## Add an object

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Open the object library">
    On the setup's row in the Outliner, click **Add an object to this setup** (the cube). The Asset Library opens on **Objects**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an object">
    Hover an object, then click **Add to Scene**. The library closes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Spark says **Click on canvas to place object. Press Esc to cancel.** Click where it goes in the 3D canvas.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prepare USD objects

Upload your own object with **Add to Library**. The **Object Settings** window asks for a subcategory and a name, and checks which way is up.

<img
  src="/images/spark/scene-editor/scale-warning.png"
  alt="Object Settings for an uploaded USDA file: Step 1 with Subcategory Prop and the name, Step 2 detecting Y-Up, and the warning Scale hasn't been adjusted with Let me check and Add it anyway."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '520px' }}
/>

- **Step 2** reads the file's up axis, such as **Detected: Y-Up**. Tick **Invert Up** or **Z-Up** if the preview is upside down or on its side.
- If you leave the scale as it is, Spark asks **Scale hasn't been adjusted. Are you sure it's right?** Click **Let me check**, or **Add it anyway**.

<Warning>
  Export every model as a mesh. Spark doesn't draw USD shapes such as Cube or Sphere. It also ignores `displayColor`, so put colour in a USD material.
</Warning>

## Move, rotate or scale

Select the object, then drag the gizmo in the 3D canvas. Switch the gizmo with the buttons at the top right of the canvas.

| Button | Shortcut |
|--------|----------|
| **Position** | **Ctrl+1** |
| **Rotation** | **Ctrl+2** |
| **Scale** | **Ctrl+3** |

To type exact values, use **Position**, **Rotation** and **Scale** in **Object Properties**. Press **Enter** or **Tab** after each value.

## Rename, replace or remove an object

- **Rename:** change **Name** in **Object Properties**. The Outliner and Timeline update.
- **Replace:** click ↻ **Replace with another file**. The library opens with **Replace** on each card. The object keeps its place and takes the new file's name.
- **Hide:** hover the object's row, then click the eye. The row turns dim. Your choice stays after a reload.
- **Remove:** click **Remove from Scene**, then **Remove**. The object leaves the scene and every setup. Its file stays in the library, and **Ctrl+Z** brings it back.

The trash on the object's row does the same as **Remove from Scene**.

Objects can't be parented to other objects. Only a camera can follow an object. See [Setups and cameras](/spark/scene-editor/setups-and-cameras).

<Note>
  ⚙ on the asset row edits the file in the library. The change applies to new placements only. Objects already in a scene keep their settings.
</Note>
