## All Takes and Lists

**All Takes** shows the takes of the open script. Pick a scene heading in the sidebar to see its 3D scenes. Cards sit under each 3D scene, then under each setup, with one card per camera.

The toolbar sets how cards show:

- **Group by**: **No grouping**, **Group by 3D Scene**, **Group by Setup** or **Group by Camera**
- Sort: **Default**, or **Date created**, newest or oldest first
- Grid or list view

**Lists** holds the lists you make. See [Create and edit a list](#create-and-edit-a-list).

## Takes and CG Takes

Every camera in a 3D scene gets a CG Take card, before you record anything. A card shows its preview, name, chips, 3D scene and setup, age and note. Click the pencil beside the name, or **Add note**, to change them.

The chips say what the card is:

- **CG TAKE**: a camera's move through the 3D scene
- **TAKE**: a recording from the Spark iOS app
- **STATIC**: the camera doesn't move, so the preview is a still
- **BG ONLY**: recorded with the phone's camera off, so the CG background is all it has

Click a preview to play it in the **Player**. Hover a card for its select box at the top left and **…** at the top right.

## Take layers

A Take card shows a chip for each video the take has: **Comp**, **BG**, **Camera**, **Matte** and **Depth**. Its download chips are **Tracking**, **Depth (raw)** and **G-matte**. A missing layer shows a dashed chip that says why.

Takes arrive once they're uploaded. See [Upload takes from iPhone](/spark/gallery/spark-desktop).

## Generate a CG Take preview

A new CG Take card reads **Generate preview** until it's rendered. When you change the 3D scene, older previews go out of date, and a yellow chip counts them.

1. In the toolbar, click **Generate N previews**. It renders the missing and out-of-date previews in the scene.
2. Wait. Two previews took about 30 seconds.

<img
  src="/images/spark/gallery/generate-previews.png"
  alt="The Gallery toolbar, with the yellow out-of-date chip reading 1, and a box on Generate 2 previews."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '440px' }}
/>

To render one card again, hover it and click the round arrow beside its **CG TAKE** chip.

## Regenerate a background

A Take recorded with the phone's camera off has no CG background. On its card's **…** menu, click **Regenerate BG**. The browser renders the background from the take's camera track and shows **Regenerating BG — frame N of M**.

It doesn't run on a take that already has a background, isn't linked to a 3D scene, or hasn't uploaded its camera track yet.

## Download takes

On a card's **…** menu, click **Download**. To download several, select them first. See [Select takes](#select-takes).

Every download is one zip named by date and time, such as `2026-10-29 20.05.04 Gallery Takes.zip`. Inside, folders go 3D scene, then setup, then camera. A CG Take is an MP4. A Take brings every layer file it has, with its tracking data.

1. In the toolbar, click **Select Takes**.
2. Tick the box on each card you want.

## Delete a take

On the card's **…** menu, click **Delete permanently**, then confirm. Deleting a CG Take removes its camera animation and any camera moves recorded against it.

<Warning>
You can't undo a delete. A CG Take that has recorded takes can't be deleted.
</Warning>

## Comment

Comments belong to a list.

1. On the list, click the round blue button at the bottom right.
2. Type in **Write a comment** and press **Enter**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/gallery/comments.png"
  alt="The Comments panel with one comment, Test comment for the docs, and its author's name blurred. A box is on the Write a comment field and its send button."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '485px' }}
/>

Each comment shows its author's name. To quote a take, click **Quote in comments** on its card's **…** menu while comments are open.

## Share a list with a link

Anyone with the link can open the list without a Spark account.

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Turn on the link">
    On the list, click **Share**, then turn on **Web**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a password and an expiry">
    Turn on **Password** and type one. Under **Expires**, pick **Never**, **1 day**, **7 days**, **30 days**, **3 months** or **1 year**, or a date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and copy the link">
    Click **Save**, then copy the link and send it.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/gallery/share-link.png"
      alt="The Share panel of P6 test list, with the end of the link blurred. Box 1 is on Share, 2 on the Web switch, 3 on the link, 4 on Password, 5 on Expires with 1 day picked, and 6 on Save."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '470px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-3 left">

    1. **Share**
    2. **Web**
    3. Link
    4. **Password**
    5. **Expires**
    6. **Save**

    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

To stop the link working, turn off **Web**. **Refresh link** makes a new link, and the old one stops working for everyone who has it.

## Open a shared list

Open the link. With a password, type it and click **Open list**.

<img
  src="/images/spark/gallery/shared-list-password.png"
  alt="A shared list asking for its password: This list is password protected. Enter the password you were given to open it. Below are the Password field and Open list."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '440px' }}
/>

The list opens read-only, with its takes, **Select Takes** and **Download all**. Comments, notes and card menus don't show.

  src="/images/spark/gallery/shared-list.jpg"
