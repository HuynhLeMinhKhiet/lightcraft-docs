---
title: 'Upload takes from iPhone'
description: 'Send takes to the Gallery from the app or with Spark Desktop, and free up the phone.'
---

Takes stay on the phone until you upload them. Upload from the app's Takes page, or offload them over USB with Spark Desktop. A take's data always syncs, so the web can render a camera-off take.

## Upload from the app

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Open Takes">
    In a 3D scene, tap the clapperboard. To upload video by itself after each take, turn on **Auto-upload video**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload">
    Tap **Upload** on a take, or **Upload All**. A bar counts the takes still uploading, and **Pause** stops them.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/gallery/upload-progress.jpg"
      alt="Takes uploading, with a box on the bar reading 2 takes uploading. Take 2 reads Queued and Take 1 reads scene_usd 1/3."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '400px' }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the cloud">
    A green tick means the take is rendered, and a green cloud means it's uploaded. Two rendered takes, 8.5 MB in all, uploaded in about a minute.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/gallery/takes-uploaded.jpg"
      alt="Both takes uploaded. Box 1 is on the green tick of Take 2, 2 on its green cloud and 3 on Free Up Space."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '400px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-3 left">

    1. Rendered
    2. Uploaded
    3. **Free Up Space**

    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

The takes then show in the [Gallery](/spark/gallery/gallery).

## Free up space

Tap **Free Up Space**, then **Remove All**. The app removes only fully uploaded takes, checking each against the cloud first. They stay in the list and can stream anytime.

## Offload takes

Spark Desktop pulls takes off the phone over USB, with every file. It installs as **Spark Atlas**. See [Install and sign in](/spark/get-started/install-and-sign-in#install-spark-desktop).

></iframe>

<img
  src="/images/spark/gallery/atlas-steps.jpg"
  alt="Spark Atlas with its four numbered steps. Box 1 is on Destination, 2 on Pull from iPhone, 3 on Organize and 4 on Upload."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-2">

1. **Destination**
2. **Pull from iPhone**
3. **Organize**
4. **Upload**

</div>

1. Plug in the phone over USB.
2. Under **Destination**, click **Choose folder…**.
3. Click **Pull takes**. Takes land in `Raw/`.
4. Click **Organize**. Takes are renamed into `Takes/`.
5. Click **Check cloud**, then **Upload all**.

<Note>
On Windows, install Apple Devices from the Microsoft Store and open it once. Without it, **Pull takes** shows **Apple Mobile Device Service not found**.
</Note>

## Send a take to Nuke or Blender

Spark Desktop can push a take's files into a set-up Nuke or Blender project. See [Autoshot overview](/autoshot/autoshot-overview) for work after Spark.
