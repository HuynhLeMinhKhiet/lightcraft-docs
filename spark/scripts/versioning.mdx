Every edit saves as a draft version that only you see. The team reads the published version until you publish your draft.

<img
  src="/images/spark/scripts/versioning-column.png"
  alt="The Versioning column in Edit mode. Marker 1 is on Publish changes, 2 on Import Newer Version, 3 on a draft version, 4 on the Imported tag and 5 on the Current Version tag."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', maxWidth: '360px' }}
/>

1. **Publish changes** appears when you have a draft to publish.
2. **Import Newer Version** brings in a new draft from a file.
3. A version, with its time and author. The newest is at the top.
4. **Imported** marks a version that came from a file.
5. **Current Version** marks the published one that everyone reads.

## The Versioning column

Switch the script to **Edit** to see it, with **Ctrl+E** (**Cmd+E** on a Mac). It lists every version of the script, newest first.

The Current Version is what everyone reads. 3D scenes, lists and downloads use it too.

## Publish your edits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your draft">
    In View mode, a draft shows as **Unpublished edits**. Click **Review & publish** to open it in Edit mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    Click **Publish changes**, then **Publish changes** again to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your draft becomes the Current Version. New scene headings join **Script scenes**, and the earlier versions stay in the list.

## Import a newer version

Use this when a new draft arrives from your screenwriting app. It adds the file as a new version, so the script keeps its history.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Versioning column">
    Switch the script to **Edit**, then click **Import Newer Version**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the file">
    Drag in a `.fountain`, `.txt`, `.fdx` or `.pdf` file, or click **Choose from browser**. Check the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import and publish">
    Click **Import**. The file becomes a draft tagged **Imported**. Publish it as you would any edit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Go back to an earlier version

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the version">
    In the **Versioning** column, click the version you want. **Version History** shows it beside the version before it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revert to it">
    Click **Revert to this version**, then **Revert**. Spark adds it as a new draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    Click **Publish changes** to make it the Current Version.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<img
  src="/images/spark/scripts/version-history.png"
  alt="The Version History window. Marker 1 is on Revert to this version, and 2 on lines shown in red because the selected version doesn't have them."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

1. **Revert to this version**.
2. Lines in red are in the version before it, but not in the one you picked.

Nothing is lost when you revert. Every version stays in the list, so you can return to any of them later.
