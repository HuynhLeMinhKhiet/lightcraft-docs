## Add a camera

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Open New Camera">
    On the setup's row in the Outliner, click **Add a camera to this setup**.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/setups-cameras/add-camera-button.png"
      alt="The Setup A row, with a box on its Add a camera to this setup button."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '268px' }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the camera">
    Name it, pick its preset, framing and subject, and say what it does in **Action**. Then click **Place Camera**.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/setups-cameras/new-camera.png"
      alt="The New Camera window for a camera on Setup A. Boxes 1 to 6 are on Camera Name, Camera Preset with the landscape and portrait toggle, Framing, Subject, Action and Place Camera."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '494px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-3 left">

    1. **Camera Name**
    2. **Camera Preset**
    3. **Framing**
    4. **Subject**
    5. **Action**
    6. **Place Camera**

    </div>

    **Framing** runs from **Extreme Wide Shot** to **Extreme Close-Up**. **Subject** lists the scene's objects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    **Click to Place** drops the camera where you click in the scene. **Match your view** places it at your current view angle.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/setups-cameras/place-camera.png"
      alt="The Place Camera window. Box 1 is on Click to Place and 2 on Match your view."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '448px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2 left">

    1. **Click to Place**
    2. **Match your view**

    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Camera Properties

Click a camera's row to open **Camera Properties**. The card at the top shows its framing, focal length and location.

<img
  src="/images/spark/setups-cameras/camera-properties.png"

  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '280px' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-3 left">

1. **Delete Camera**
2. Edit the camera
3. Preset
4. **Focal Length**

6. **Focus Distance**

The pencil opens **Edit Camera** to change those details. Click **Update Camera** to save.

## Set the lens

Pick a body and sensor in the preset list, and landscape or portrait beside it. Then set the lens values:

- **Focal Length**, in mm. The camera preview changes as you type.
- **T-Stop**, from 2.2 to 22.
- **Focus Distance**, in meters, up to 60. A new camera starts at -1.0.

A fixed lens shows **T-Stop** and **Focus Distance** grayed out.

## Place and frame

Enter the camera's **Position** and **Rotation** in Camera Properties, and watch the camera preview. Press **Shift+F** to enlarge the preview over the canvas, and again to shrink it.

## Aim at a target

**Aim At** keeps the camera pointed at its subject.

1. Give the camera a **Subject**: click the pencil, choose an object, then **Update Camera**.
2. Click **Aim At**. The camera turns to the subject.

<img
  src="/images/spark/setups-cameras/camera-aim.png"
  alt="Camera Properties, with a box on the Aim At button, which is on, and Aim At Offset below it."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '290px' }}
/>

**Aim At Offset** shifts the aim point.

## Parent to an object or part

A parented camera follows the object's motion. Open **Parent** and choose an object, or one of its parts, marked **↳**. Choose **None (world)** to set it free.

<img
  src="/images/spark/setups-cameras/camera-parent.png"
  alt="The Parent list, open. Box 1 is on the list, 2 on the parts Shade and Pole under P5 desk lamp mesh, and 3 on the eyedropper."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '290px' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-3 left">

1. **Parent**
2. Parts
3. Pick in the canvas

</div>

To pick in the canvas instead, click the eyedropper, then click what the camera should follow. See [Parts of an object](/spark/scene-editor/parts-of-an-object).

## Change duration

A camera runs from its first keyframe to its last, shown as a bar on its Timeline row. To make it longer, add a keyframe later on. See [Animate with keyframes](/spark/setups-cameras/timeline-and-animation#animate-with-keyframes).

## Track script coverage

Link a camera to the script lines it covers.

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Open the script">
    In Camera Properties, click **Link script lines**. The Script tab opens.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/setups-cameras/link-script-lines.png"
      alt="The Script section of Camera Properties, with a box on Link script lines."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '280px' }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the lines">
    Drag across the lines, then click **Update Camera**.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/setups-cameras/link-lines-select.png"
      alt="The Script tab with the line Maya runs down the stairs selected. Box 1 is on the line and 2 on Update Camera."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '290px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2 left">

    1. Selected line
    2. **Update Camera**

    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

The lines then show under **Script** in Camera Properties, with a pencil to change them.

## Delete a camera

Click **Delete Camera** in Camera Properties, then **Delete**. Its keyframes go with it, and **Ctrl+Z** brings it back.
