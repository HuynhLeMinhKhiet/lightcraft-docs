## Play the scene

Click play or press **Space**. The playhead runs across the ruler, and animated objects move in the canvas.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **Space** | Play or pause |
| **←** / **→** | One frame back or forward |
| **Shift+←** / **Shift+→** | 1 second back or forward |
| **Shift+↑** | Jump to the start |
| **Shift+↓** | Jump to the end, or the selected camera's last keyframe |
| **Alt+↑** / **Alt+↓** | Zoom the Timeline in or out |

## Animate with keyframes

A keyframe holds where a camera or an object is at one time.

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Turn on Auto-keyframe">
    Select the camera or object. Under **Transformation**, turn on **Auto-keyframe**. Spark places a keyframe at the playhead.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/setups-cameras/auto-keyframe.png"
      alt="The Transformation section. Box 1 is on Auto-keyframe, switched on, 2 on Delete Keyframe and 3 on the interpolation menu."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '290px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-3 left">

    1. **Auto-keyframe**
    2. Keyframe button
    3. Interpolation

    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move the playhead">
    Press **Shift+→** to move 1 second later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the position">
    Enter a new **Position** or **Rotation**. Spark adds a keyframe at the playhead.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/setups-cameras/position-fields.png"
      alt="The Position section, with a box on its X, Y and Z fields."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '280px' }}
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>

The keyframe button reads **Place Keyframe**, or **Delete Keyframe** when the playhead is on one. The menu beside it sets how the move eases between keyframes, such as **Smooth**.

## Add a pre-animated USD clip

A USD file with its own animation brings it in as a clip. When you upload it, **Object Settings** shows the animation's length.

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Add the object">
    Add it to the setup like any object. See [Add an object](/spark/scene-editor/objects#add-an-object).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the clip">
    Expand the object's row in the Outliner. Move the playhead to where the clip starts, then click **+** on the **Animation** row.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/setups-cameras/animation-node.png"
      alt="The Outliner with p5-sliding-box expanded. Box 1 is on its row and 2 on the plus button of its Animation row, 2.0s long."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '290px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2 left">

    1. Object row
    2. **Add to timeline at playhead**

    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

The clip shows on the object's Timeline row. Its properties gain **Animation**, with **Remove Animation**.

## Move a clip

Drag a clip along its row to start it at another time. Dragging an end moves the whole clip, so clips can't be trimmed here. To trim a sound, see [Add audio](/spark/scene-editor/audio-locators-and-rulers#add-audio).
