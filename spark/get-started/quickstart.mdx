---
title: 'Quickstart'
description: 'Make your first 3D scene in a few minutes, from the sample script to a CG Take.'
---

You need a production you can open. To create one, see [Create a production](/spark/organizations-productions/productions#create-a-production).

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Add the sample script">
    In your production, open **Scripts** and click **Use default script**. If the production already has scripts, click **Add a Script** first. Spark lists the 14 scene headings under **Script scenes**.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/get-started/quickstart-default-script.png"
      alt="The Scripts page with the Add a Script menu open. Box 1 is on Add a Script and 2 on Use default script."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2">

    1. **Add a Script**
    2. **Use default script**

    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a 3D scene">
    Click **INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY**, then **Create a new 3D scene**. Keep the name and click **Create**.

    <img src="/images/spark/get-started/quickstart-create-3d-scene.png" alt="The Create 3D Scene window, with Camera Preset, Landscape or Portrait, and the Name field filled with INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY. A box is on Create." style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '520px' }} />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a location">
    Click the new 3D scene to open it. In the Outliner, click **+** on the **Locations** row. Point at **Opulent Hall** and click **Import & Add**.

    <img src="/images/spark/get-started/quickstart-add-location.jpg" alt="The Asset Library with Locations selected. A box is on Import and Add over the Opulent Hall card." style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />

    The location can take half a minute to appear, and longer to load.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Frame the camera">
    Click **Camera 1** in the Outliner. Change **Focal Length** and watch the camera preview.

    <img src="/images/spark/get-started/quickstart-frame-camera.jpg" alt="Opulent Hall in the scene editor. Box 1 is on Camera 1 in the Outliner, 2 on Focal Length set to 35 and 3 on the camera preview." style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />

    <div className="key cols-3">

    1. **Camera 1**
    2. **Focal Length**
    3. Camera preview

    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the CG Take">
    Open the **Gallery** tab and click **INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY**. On the **Camera 1** card, click **Generate preview**.

    <img src="/images/spark/get-started/quickstart-cg-take.png" alt="The Camera 1 card in the Gallery, showing the hall at 35mm. A box is on the CG Take tag." style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '320px' }} />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You have a 3D scene with a location, a camera and a CG Take.
</Check>
