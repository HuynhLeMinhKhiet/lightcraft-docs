Spark has three apps. You plan in the web app, shoot with the iPhone or iPad app, and bring takes onto your computer with Spark Desktop.

| App | Runs on | What it's for |
|-----|---------|---------------|
| Spark web app | Google Chrome | Scripts, 3D scenes, cameras, the Gallery and the Editor |
| Spark iOS app | iPhone and iPad | Shooting takes inside a 3D scene |
| Spark Desktop | Mac and Windows | Moving takes from the iPhone or iPad to the Gallery |

## Sign in on the web

Open [spark.lightcraft.pro](https://spark.lightcraft.pro) in Chrome. Use the email address your invitation went to.

**Continue with Email** signs in with an email and password. It also has **Sign up** and **Forgot password?**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Spark says Email Not Whitelisted">
    Spark lets in approved email addresses only. An invitation to an organization doesn't add your address. Write to [support@lightcraft.pro](mailto:support@lightcraft.pro) to ask for access, then sign in again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Install and sign in on iPhone or iPad

Sign in on the web first, so your account exists before the app opens. The Home page then shows a QR code under **Shoot with Spark on iPhone**.

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Get the app">
    Scan the QR code with your iPhone, or open [Lightcraft Spark](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lightcraft-spark/id6761484818) on the App Store.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/get-started/install-qr-code.jpg"
      alt="The Shoot with Spark on iPhone banner on the Home page, with a box on its QR code."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the app and sign in with the same account as on the web. See [Spark iOS app](/spark/spark-ios/spark-ios-app#open-a-3d-scene).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Install Spark Desktop

Spark Desktop installs as **Spark Atlas**. Download it from **Download Spark Desktop** in the Gallery, or here:

- [Spark Desktop for Windows](https://downloads.lightcraft.pro/lightcraftsync/download/windows)
- [Spark Desktop for macOS](https://downloads.lightcraft.pro/lightcraftsync/download/mac)

To sign in, open Spark Atlas and click **Sign in** at the top right. Your browser opens to finish signing in.

See [Upload takes from iPhone](/spark/gallery/spark-desktop#offload-takes) for moving takes off the device.
