              }} />

            <div style={{ display: 'flex', alignItems: 'baseline', gap: '0.35rem' }}>

Spark is a web app where a film crew plans a shoot before anyone arrives on set. You turn a script into 3D scenes, plan the cameras, shoot with an iPhone or iPad, then review and cut the takes.

<StageStrip />

## How Spark is organized

| Item | Holds |
|------|-------|
| Organization | Members, billing and every production |
| Production | Scripts, the Asset Library, the Gallery and edits |
| Script | One script scene for each scene heading |
| 3D scene | Setups, cameras and takes for one script scene |

The tabs at the bottom of a production follow the same order: **Scripts**, **Asset Library**, **3D Scene**, **Gallery** and **Editor**.

## Spark, Jetset and Autoshot

Spark is where a Lightcraft production starts. Jetset tracks the cinema camera on set, and Autoshot brings that tracking into Blender, Unreal, After Effects or Nuke. Click **Export** in the scene editor to send a 3D scene to Jetset.
