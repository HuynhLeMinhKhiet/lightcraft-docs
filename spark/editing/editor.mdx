The **CLIP** viewer plays the take or clip you pick. The **PROGRAM** viewer plays the edit. The timeline has a **Video** lane, V1, and an **Audio** lane, A1. Click **+** beside **Video** or **Audio** to add a lane.

## Create an edit

1. Click the **Editor** tab at the bottom of the production.
2. Click **New edit**. It opens as **Untitled Edit**, 1920 × 1080 at 24 fps.

<img
  src="/images/spark/editing/editor-edits.jpg"
  alt="The Edits list. Box 1 is on New edit, 2 on the P6 test edit card with its length, clip count and last export, and 3 on its … button."
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
/>

<div className="key cols-3">

1. **New edit**
2. Edit
3. **…**

</div>

To rename an edit, change **Name** when you export it. See [Export an edit](#export-an-edit).

## Cut takes into an edit

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Pick where the takes come from">
    On the **Takes** tab, open the picker. It lists every script and every list in the production, and **New list…**.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/editing/editor-source-picker.png"
      alt="The Takes tab picker open. Box 1 is on the picker reading P6 test list, 2 on P6 test list in the menu, and 3 on New list…."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '320px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-3 left">

    1. Picker
    2. A list
    3. **New list…**

    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the part you want">
    Click a take to load it in the **CLIP** viewer. Move the playhead and click **Mark in**, then move it again and click **Mark out**. A still from a **STATIC** CG Take can't be marked.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put it on the timeline">
    Click **Insert** to add it at the playhead and push later clips along. Click **Overwrite** to cover what's there. You can also drag a take onto a lane.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/editing/editor-cut.png"
      alt="The CLIP viewer controls under Camera 2, with the marked part showing on the scrub bar. Box 1 is on Mark in, 2 on Mark out, 3 on Insert and 4 on Overwrite."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '560px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-2 left">

    1. **Mark in**
    2. **Mark out**
    3. **Insert**
    4. **Overwrite**

    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Without marks, **Insert** adds the whole take. A still goes in 5 seconds long.

The timeline toolbar splits a clip at the playhead, trims its start or end, and removes clips. **Close every gap** pulls the clips together. Press **Ctrl** and scroll to zoom the timeline.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Editor keys">
    | Key | Does |
    |---|---|
    | Space | Play or pause |
    | ← or → | One frame back or forward |
    | Shift+← or Shift+→ | 1 second back or forward |
    | Shift+↑ or Shift+↓ | The start, or the end of the last clip |
    | I or O | Mark in or Mark out |
    | Alt+X | Clear the marks |
    | V or B | Insert or Overwrite |
    | Ctrl+K | Split |
    | Q or W | Trim the start or the end |
    | G | Close a gap |
    | Delete | Remove the selection (Backspace on a Mac) |
    | Ctrl+Z or Ctrl+Shift+Z | Undo or redo |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Choose which video a clip plays

A clip cut from a Take can play any of its videos: **Comp**, **BG**, **Camera**, **Matte** or **Depth**. A CG Take is one video.

Select a clip to see its properties in the inspector. **Shift+I** shows or hides it. The inspector sets the clip's framing, rotates it 90° left or right, or mutes it. A clip hidden under a higher lane is flagged **It will not appear in the export.**

## Add sound

1. Click the **Audio** tab. To add a file, click **Upload**.
2. On a sound's **…** menu, click **Add at playhead**. It goes on the audio lane.

## Export an edit

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Open the builds">
    At the top, click **Export**, then **New export**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the settings">
    Set the name, aspect ratio, resolution and frame rate. These change the edit itself as well as this build. **This build** covers the whole timeline. To build part of it, drag a loop band on the ruler first.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/editing/editor-new-export.png"
      alt="The New export window. Box 1 is on Name, 2 on Aspect ratio and Resolution, 3 on Frame rate, 4 on This build and 5 on Build."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '500px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-3 left">

    1. **Name**
    2. **Aspect ratio** and **Resolution**
    3. **Frame rate**
    4. **This build**
    5. **Build**

    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build it">
    Click **Build**. An 11-second edit took about 18 seconds. The build joins the **Builds** list.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/editing/editor-builds.png"
      alt="The Export view of P6 test edit. Box 1 is on Export, 2 on New export and 3 on the finished build: 0:11, 1920 × 1080, 16:9, 24 fps, 3.9 MB, 4 clips."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '980px' }}
    />

    <div className="key cols-3">

    1. **Export**
    2. **New export**
    3. Build

    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download it">
    On the build's **…** menu, click **Download**. The file is an MP4.

    <img
      src="/images/spark/editing/editor-build-menu.png"
      alt="The … menu of a build. Box 1 is on Download, 2 on Rename and 3 on Delete."
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', width: '100%', maxWidth: '260px' }}
    />

    <div className="key left">

    1. **Download**
    2. **Rename**
    3. **Delete**

    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

A build that fails or is canceled leaves nothing in the list.
